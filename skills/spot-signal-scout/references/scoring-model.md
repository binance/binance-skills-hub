# Scoring Model

| Factor | Maximum |
|---|---:|
| Market Structure | 25 |
| Volume & Participation | 20 |
| Momentum | 15 |
| Confirmation & Volatility | 15 |
| Risk/Reward | 15 |
| Liquidity & Market Quality | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

The score measures the quality of the technical evidence available in the supplied market data. It is not a probability of success, expected return, or recommendation.

A score of 80+ is not enough by itself to classify a market configuration as confirmed. The analytical classification also requires adequate structure, volume, confirmation evidence, illustrative R:R >= 2.0, acceptable liquidity, adequate data freshness, and no hard rejection.

Illustrative R:R is a mathematical comparison of reference levels identified during analysis. It is not a recommended trade, entry instruction, exit instruction, or prediction of future returns.

Keep raw score, confidence, and final analytical decision state separate.
