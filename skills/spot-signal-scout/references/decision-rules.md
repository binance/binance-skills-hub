# Decision Rules

Priority order:

1. Data validity
2. Disqualifying conditions
3. Market regime
4. Structure
5. Volume/participation
6. Momentum
7. Confirmation
8. Liquidity
9. Score
10. Confidence
11. Analytical state

Structure has priority over isolated indicators.

Do not average contradictory evidence into false confidence.

All outputs are descriptive. Do not convert an analytical state into an instruction to act.
